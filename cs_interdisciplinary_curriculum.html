              📖 Methodology — How Each Parameter Is Scored
              Market Size: India market value by 2030. 10 = $80B+ (Cloud); 1 = below $0.5B (Quantum today).
              Growth (CAGR): Compound annual growth rate. 10 = 25%+ (AI, Healthcare); 1 = under 7% (AgriTech).
              Salary: Senior tier-1 ceiling in India. 10 = ₹60+ LPA (AI, Cloud); 5 = ~₹20 LPA; 1 = under ₹10 LPA.
              Job Volume: Sheer count of jobs created/projected. 10 = 1M+ openings (AI, Cloud, DS); 1 = niche (Quantum).
              Talent Gap: Demand-supply mismatch. 10 = 50%+ unfilled (AI, Cloud, Quantum); 1 = oversupplied.
              Govt Push: Strength & funding of national missions. 10 = dedicated ₹6,000+ Cr mission (AI, Semicon, Quantum); 1 = minimal.
              Startup Potential: Active startup ecosystem & unicorn pipeline. 10 = 3000+ startups (FinTech, AI); 1 = nascent.
              Global Demand: Job demand in US/UK/EU/APAC. 10 = universal demand (AI, Cyber, Cloud); 1 = India-centric.
              Hiring Mix: Diversity of recruiting industries. 10 = every sector hires (AI, Cyber); 1 = single-industry.
              Future-Proof: 10-year forward relevance. 10 = becomes infrastructure (AI, Cyber, Health); 1 = uncertain.
              Curriculum Maturity: Templates & references available. 10 = well-established (CSBS, AI, DS); 1 = experimental (Quantum, LegalTech).
              Intl Mobility: Visa-friendliness & global hiring. 10 = STEM-OPT/Blue Card eligible (AI, Cyber); 1 = restricted (Defense).
              Research Depth: PhD pipeline, publications, fundamental science. 10 = deep theory (AI, Quantum, Robotics); 1 = applied only.
              Nat. Priority: Direct alignment with mission documents. 10 = flagship mission (AI, Semicon, ABDM, NQM); 1 = peripheral.
              Infra Access: Inverse of capex required. 10 = laptop + cloud sufficient (Cloud, Web3); 1 = needs lab > ₹3 Cr (Robotics, Aero, Manuf).
              Faculty Avail: Availability of PhD-qualified faculty. 10 = readily available (CSBS, DS); 1 = severe shortage (Quantum, AgriTech).

              🔬 Research Dual Degrees — Models & Recommendation

              IIT Madras IDDD (5 yrs)

              Interdisciplinary Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech): Data Science, Cyber-Physical Systems, Robotics, Computational Engineering; Year-5 = thesis. Source: askiitm.com, iitm.ac.in.

              IISc B.Tech (Math & Computing) + Integrated M.Tech

              IISc Bengaluru research-first culture; integrated programs require 1-year thesis with publication. (iisc.ac.in)

              IIIT Hyderabad / IIIT-Bangalore

              B.Tech + MS by Research dual — 5-yr program with mandatory MS thesis and ≥1 publication. IIITH offers CSD, CSE, ECE, CL, CND streams.

              BITS Pilani / Plaksha

              BITS: 5-yr integrated M.Sc.+B.E. dual. Plaksha: B.Tech in CS & AI, Robotics & CPS, Data Science with industry capstone.

              Recommended Hybrid for a new institution: A 5-year B.Tech (CS-Domain) + MS by Research: (a) common 3-yr B.Tech CS core; (b) Year 4 = lab-rotation + qualifying review; (c) Year 5 = full-time thesis with mandatory 1 conference paper or 1 filed patent; (d) Exit option after Year 4 with B.Tech (Hons.) per NEP MEME.

              🧑‍🏫 Pedagogical Recommendations

              Project-Based Learning (PBL)

              ≥40% of credits via PBL with real datasets and industry-supplied problems; weekly studio-style critique.

              Mandatory Internships

              Min 8 weeks after Sem 6 + full Sem 8 (6 months) industry internship — counted as 18-credit capstone. NEP 2020-aligned.

              MOOC Integration

              Up to 20% of credits via NPTEL-SWAYAM, Coursera, edX. Mandatory 4 NPTEL elite-grade certificates per student.

              Honors & Minor

              20-credit Minor (cross-disciplinary) and 18-credit Honors (research-intensive) per AICTE 2024 4-year UG framework.

              International Collaboration

              Semester-abroad MoUs with NUS, ETH Zurich, Purdue, ASU, NTU — under AICTE Twinning & Joint Degree regulations 2022.

              Hackathons & Industry Visits

              2 hackathons/yr (Smart India Hackathon mandatory), 4 industry visits, monthly CXO/Founder talks.

              Research Paper & Patent Culture

              Each student exits with ≥1 conference paper or patent disclosure; integrated with Honors thesis.

              Faculty Capability Building

              TCS Train-the-Trainer model + AICTE ATAL FDPs + 2-week annual industry immersion for every faculty member.

              🏆 Accreditation Strategy (NBA · NAAC · ABET)

              NBA Tier-1 (mandatory)

              All new CS-X programs should target NBA Tier-1 within 3 years. Requires Outcome-Based Education (OBE), CO-PO mapping, ≥75% Ph.D. faculty in core, structured CQI processes.

              NAAC A++ (institutional)

              NEP-2020 multidisciplinary framework, ABC (Academic Bank of Credits), MoUs with industry & foreign universities, ≥30% research/publication output.

              ABET (aspirational, USA)

              For 2-3 flagship programs (CS, CS+AI). Improves student mobility to US grad schools and global recruiter perception.

              NIRF Ranking

              Target NIRF Engineering top-100 in 5 yrs, top-50 in 10 yrs. Drivers: Teaching-Learning Resources, Research, Graduation Outcomes, Outreach, Perception.

              Sector Body Endorsements

              NASSCOM FutureSkills Prime mapping; CII industry endorsement; sector skill councils (IT-ITeS SSC, BFSI SSC, Electronics SSC).

              International Accreditation Pathways

              Washington Accord (via NBA), EUR-ACE (Europe), Engineers Canada — for graduate mobility.

              ⚠️ Challenges & Implementation Considerations

              Faculty Shortage

              India faces 40k+ cybersecurity, 600k AI talent shortfall (NASSCOM–Deloitte 2024). PhD-qualified faculty in Quantum, Blockchain, Robotics, GenAI extremely scarce. Plan industry-PhD bridge and visiting-faculty pools.

              Lab & Infra Capex

              Per-track capex: AI ≈ ₹1.5–3 Cr (GPU cluster); Robotics ≈ ₹2–4 Cr; FinTech ≈ ₹50L (Bloomberg/Reuters terminals); Quantum ≈ pay-per-use cloud (AWS Braket/Qiskit Runtime).

              Industry Partnerships

              Replicate TCS-BS MoU (now 55+ colleges, 5,000+ students) for each domain — TCS, Infosys, IBM, Intel, NVIDIA, Microsoft, AWS, Wipro, NPCI, HDFC, Apollo, Tata Elxsi, Mahindra Defence.

              Accreditation

              Mandatory NBA Tier-1, NAAC A++, plus aspirational ABET (USA) for select streams. AICTE Model Curriculum 2024 + NEP 2020 MEME must be built-in from day 1.

              Skill-Gap Realities

              AI demand-supply gap ~50% (2024); Cybersecurity gap ~30% with 40k open roles; Cloud/DevOps 64% of firms struggle to hire — implies high placement potential but also pricing pressure on faculty.

              Curriculum Refresh Cadence

              GenAI changed CS jobs profile in 18 months. Set up a Curriculum Advisory Board with CXO members + biannual syllabus refresh. AICTE allows ≤30% modification per cycle without re-approval.
