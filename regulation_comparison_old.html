Executive Overview

VIT operates under the Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS™) — a slot-based, choice-driven, relative-grading framework introduced in 2008 and now in its 4th major version. REC AR-23 follows the more conventional UGC/AICTE CBCS framework, aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP-2020), with mandatory mid-sem tests, absolute grading and a fixed year-wise curriculum. The two regulations therefore differ on almost every operational dimension: course registration, evaluation weightages, grading philosophy (relative vs absolute), attendance enforcement, classification of the degree, and award structure.

2. Credit Requirements & Distribution

Total Credits and Course Buckets

DIFFERENT
VIT
  • Total credits = curriculum-set per programme; no fixed all-programmes number stated in regulations.
  • 1 credit = 15 hrs lecture / 15 hrs tutorial / 30 hrs lab. LTPC notation (e.g., 2-1-2-4).
  • Course buckets: Foundation Core (FC), Discipline Linked Engineering (DLEC), Discipline Core (DC), Discipline Elective (DE) / Specialization Elective (SE), Open Elective (OE), Skill Enhancement (SEC), Ability Enhancement (AEC), Value Added, English/Foreign Language, Compulsory, Optional, Honours, Project & Internship.

REC AR-23
  • Fixed 160 credits for B.Tech (8 semesters).
  • Lateral entry students complete 120 credits in 3 years (start Sem-III).
  • Distribution buckets per AICTE: Basic Sciences (BS), Engineering Sciences (ES), Programme Core (PC), Programme Electives (PE), Open Electives (OE), Humanities & Social Sciences (HSS), Skill courses, Projects, Internships, Mandatory non-credit courses.

5. Course Registration / Add-Drop / Withdrawal

Registration Mechanics

VIT IS UNIQUE
VIT — Choice-Based Registration
  • Mandatory Course Registration on declared days; late registration up to 10 days with fee + Dean approval.
  • Add/Drop permitted in first 3 instructional days; not available in summer/weekend.
  • Course Withdrawal ("W"): after CAT-I if dissatisfied with performance, 3-day window; must keep ≥16 credits (graduating year exempt). Re-registered courses cannot be withdrawn. Fee charged.

  • Pre-requisites, anti-requisites, co-requisites codified per course; F-grade in pre-req still allows higher-level registration (deemed exposure).
REC AR-23

AR-23 does not feature a flexible per-semester registration mechanism — students are enrolled in their year's fixed curriculum. Re-registration of failed courses follows supplementary norms.

15. Pass Criteria

Minimum Marks to Clear a Course

DIFFERENT
VIT
  • Theory FAT: ≥40% of FAT alone.
  • Lab / Project component: ≥50% of component (average across exercises).
  • Grand total (in credit ratio for 100): ≥50% (except theory-only courses).
  • Theory-only courses: pass is set by the relative grading band (Mean − 2σ floor).
  • No minimum mark on CAM.
  • If E-grade band falls below 50, that band sets the pass mark; if above 50, students above absolute 50 still passed as "E".
REC AR-23
  • End-Sem: ≥35% (≥24.5/70).
  • Internal + External combined: ≥40% (≥40/100).
  • Both required.
  • Mandatory non-credit: ≥40% in 30-mark internal evaluation.

17. SGPA & CGPA Formulas

Computation

SAME FORMULA · MINOR DIFFERENCES
VIT

GPA = Σ(Cᵢ × GPᵢ) / Σ Cᵢ per semester. CGPA same across all semesters since joining.

  • Failed-course credits counted only once, even after re-attempts.
  • Excluded: P-grade, Audit, Honours, Minor, W courses.
  • GPA/CGPA rounded to 2 decimals.
  • Programme rank by CGPA; ties share rank, next rank skips.
REC AR-23

SGPA = Σ(Cᵢ × GPᵢ) / Σ Cᵢ; CGPA = same across all semesters.

  • "AB" / "F" carry 0 grade points.
  • Honors credits excluded from B.Tech CGPA computation.

18. CGPA → Percentage Conversion

Conversion Formula

DIFFERENT
VIT

Percentage = CGPA × 10. Example: 8.75 → 87.5%. CoE issues conversion certificate.

REC AR-23

Percentage = (CGPA − 0.5) × 10. Example: 8.75 → 82.5%. Standard JNTU / AP autonomous formula.

20. Attendance Requirements

Minimum Attendance & Condonation

DIFFERENT
VIT
  • Expected 100%; permitted minimum 75% per course, per exam component (CAT-I, CAT-II, FAT).
  • 25% absence covers all reasons (medical, personal, NCC/NSS, events) — no proof required.
  • Falling below 75% before any component ⇒ "Debarred"; absent at FAT ⇒ "N" grade → re-register.
  • 9-point CGPA exemption: CGPA ≥ 9.00 + no backlog → exempt for that exam (status recomputed dynamically). Not available in summer/weekend.
  • Beyond 25% absence (prolonged illness) ⇒ apply for Break of Study.
  • Sectional holidays for Meelad-un-Nabi, Muharram, Bakrid, Onam, Telugu New Year, Good Friday — requests through Proctor.
  • No condonation fee structure mentioned.
  • Benchmark-disability students may get +30 min CAT / +60 min FAT at Dean/CoE discretion.
REC AR-23
  • 40% minimum per course + 75% aggregate required for promotion.
  • Condonation rules per JNTU-GV norms (typically 65-74% with medical proof + prescribed fee).
  • Attendance counts toward aggregate for mandatory non-credit courses.
  • Shortfall = detention from end-sem exams; re-registration in subsequent semester required.

21. Promotion Rules

Year-to-Year Promotion

DIFFERENT
VIT

REC AR-23

23. Re-Registration / Supplementary Exams

Backlog Handling

DIFFERENT
VIT
  • "F" grade ⇒ re-register the whole course (all components reset); fee per university norms.
  • "N" grade ⇒ if some components cleared, only failed component(s) re-registered.
  • Re-registration possible in Fall / Winter / Weekend Intra / Summer semesters.
  • Arrear examination option available after final semester (must register all components; no component-only).
  • Grade Improvement (Sec 15): one re-registration per course; max 1 per regular semester (more in graduating / timed-out year).
  • Course Substitution: failed DE / OE replaceable by another in same basket; not allowed for FC/DC/DLEC.
  • Re-evaluation of FAT: deviation >5% → 3rd evaluator; best of two/three; ≤5% → best of two. No re-evaluation for labs/viva/project/comprehensive/soft-skill.
  • Further dispute → Examination Appellate Committee; their marks final.
REC AR-23
  • Supplementary exams offered each semester for "F" / "AB".
  • Mandatory non-credit courses: re-exam available on failure.
  • Internal (mid-sem) marks generally carry forward; re-registration when supplementary attempts exhausted.

  • Each case enquired by a "Malpractice Committee" constituted by the University.
  • CAT-level malpractice → zero marks in all subjects of that semester's CAT.
  • FAT-level malpractice → "N" grade in all FAT-eligible courses of that semester; student must re-register.
  • Submitting bought/borrowed/duplicate project → "N" + malpractice; re-register.
  • "N" grade also awarded for acts of indiscipline (regardless of academic performance).
  • "Outstanding dues" → registration permitted but classes barred until fees paid.
REC AR-23 — Discipline & Anti-Ragging
  • Discipline governed by the College Code of Conduct.
  • Detailed statutory anti-ragging penalties under AP Legislative Assembly Act 26 of 1997 (see Section 36 below).
  • Indiscipline / malpractice handled by the Examination Committee & Disciplinary Committee per JNTU-GV norms.
  • Mandatory anti-ragging undertaking signed at admission.

Key Takeaways at a Glance

  1. Philosophy — VIT is choice-driven and flexible (FFCS + CAL); REC is structured and NEP-aligned (CBCS, multiple entry-exit).
  2. Evaluation weightage — VIT places 60% on continuous assessment and 40% on the end exam; REC reverses this (30 : 70). VIT's continuous portion is assignment-heavy (30 marks); REC's is mid-test-heavy.
  3. Grading — VIT uses class-wise relative grading for theory (with absolute fallback); REC uses pure absolute grading. VIT has no class/division; REC awards Distinction / First / Second / Pass.
  4. Pass mark — VIT: ≥40% in FAT alone and ≥50% overall (theory-only graded relatively). REC: ≥35% end exam and ≥40% overall.
  5. CGPA → % — VIT × 10; REC (CGPA − 0.5) × 10 (5-point delta).
  6. Attendance — VIT 75% per course (with 9-CGPA exemption); REC 40% per course + 75% aggregate.
  7. Honours / Minor — VIT requires 18 + 18 extra credits (≥7.5 CGPA). REC offers +15 Honors and 12-credit Minor within 160 — more accessible.
  8. Internship volume — REC mandates three (CSP, II, Final-Sem 200-mark capstone); VIT requires only 4-week industrial + final-semester project.
  9. NEP entry/exit — Formal in REC; not codified in VIT FFCS v4.0.
  10. Unique to VIT: FFCS, slot timetable, CAL, Proctor, SAP, Honours Club, Hack-a-thon bonus marks, weekend/summer semesters, course substitution & equivalence, relative grading, re-FAT, Examination Appellate Committee.
  11. Unique to REC: NEP multi-exit, ABC, 25% blended learning, mandatory MOOC, statutory anti-ragging penalties, codified drawing/skill evaluation, three mandatory internships, 3-week induction, mandatory audit courses with re-exam, AP-formula CGPA conversion.
